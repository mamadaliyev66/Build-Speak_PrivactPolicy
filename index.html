Privacy Policy for Build&Speak

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Build&Speak ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Build&Speak mobile application and related services.

By using Build&Speak, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Build&Speak.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Build&Speak may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, username, or other information you provide when creating an account.
  • Learning and app activity: such as progress, completed lessons, exercises, preferences, achievements, and other interactions with the app.
  • Voice and microphone data: when you choose to use speaking, pronunciation, or voice-based features, the app may request access to your device microphone in order to process your speech.
  • Technical information: such as device type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, crash information, and diagnostic data.
  • Usage information: such as how features are used, session activity, and app performance information.
  • Support information: information you provide when contacting us for customer support or reporting an issue.

2. Microphone and Voice Features

Build&Speak may request microphone permission when you use features involving pronunciation practice, speaking exercises, speech recognition, or similar functionality.

Microphone access is used only when required for the feature you choose to use. You may disable microphone permission at any time through your device settings, although certain speaking features may no longer function correctly.

If voice recordings or speech data are transmitted to a third-party speech recognition or artificial intelligence service, they may be processed by that service in order to provide the requested feature.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Build&Speak.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save learning progress and preferences.
  • Provide speaking and pronunciation features.
  • Personalize the learning experience.
  • Improve app functionality and performance.
  • Detect bugs, crashes, fraud, or security problems.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Third-Party Services

Some Build&Speak features may use third-party services, including artificial intelligence, speech recognition, authentication, cloud hosting, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

When necessary to provide a requested feature, limited information may be transmitted to these service providers. These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

We do not authorize third-party service providers to use personal information for purposes unrelated to providing their services to Build&Speak, except where permitted or required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.
